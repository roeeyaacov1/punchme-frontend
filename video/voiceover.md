# The promo video: script and voiceover

43.5 seconds, 1080 × 1920 at 30 fps. Every word is on screen, so the video
works on mute; the voiceover below is for a recording laid over it later. It
says what the screen says, a little more fully, in the same order and time.
A line may run up to a second into the next beat; it should never run ahead
of the screen.

The render itself has no sound. Timings are from the start of the video and
match `BEATS` in `src/Promo.tsx` and the timings at the top of each scene.

## For whoever records it

- Calm and plain, the way the site reads. No music swell, no hype, no "amazing".
- Hebrew in the plural, as the site speaks: התחילו, not התחל.
- "₪99" is read as a price, תשעים ותשעה שקלים / ninety-nine shekels, and
  the figure as פי שישה / six times. Neither is ever said as a promise of what
  PunchMe will do: the figure is about regulars in general.
- If the SMS code is off when the video goes out (`SMS_CODE` in
  `src/config.ts`), the join's line drops "and the code from a text" on
  screen, and the voiceover drops it too.
